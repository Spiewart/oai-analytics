# Steps and Incident/Progressive Knee OA Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Test whether GT1M steps/day at 48 months, and their change to 72 months, predict incident and progressive knee OA, and whether incident or progressing OA predicts later steps.

**Architecture:** A new analysis, `analyses/steps_oa/`, built like `crepitus_activity`, with two steps:
- `cohort` (Python): writes a person frame and knee-level outcome frames to OAI_FRAME_DIR, plus an aggregate flow and descriptives.
  - Device measures come from `oai.derive.devices.person_wave`.
  - Knee outcomes come from `oai.derive.incident_soa`, called once per side (Luyten's `for_side` pattern) with `outcomes.roa_source = "outcomes99"`.
- `models` (R): the forward models P1, P2 and S1–S4 and the reverse models R1–R3.

**Tech Stack:** Python ≥ 3.11 (uv, polars, pytest), R (renv, geepack, survival, splines, `oaimodels`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-steps-oa-design.md`

- **Data stays out of git.** No OAI data in git. Step files come from `OAI_STEPS_DIR` only. Participant-level frames go to OAI_FRAME_DIR. Results are aggregates only.
- **Device rules copied verbatim from `walking_steps`:** every `device.*`, `steps.day_rule` and `sample.min_step_days`. A test fails if they drift, as in `tests/test_crepitus_activity_definition.py`.
- **Outcome keys copied from `crepitus_soa_longterm`:**
  - every `outcomes.*` key and `cohort.reading_project`;
  - a test compares value, status and choices, so they cannot drift;
  - `periods.horizon` defaults to `"10"` here.
- **Steps are scaled per 1,000/day.** The step bands are fixed: `lt5000` (< 5,000, the reference), `5000_7499`, `7500_9999` and `ge10000`.
- **Change** = the 72-month value minus the 48-month value.
- **Models:**
  - `m1` = age, sex, BMI and wear hours at each device wave the model spans (reuse `covariates_for` from crepitus_activity's `covariates.R`, copied into this analysis);
  - `m2` = `m1` plus that knee's 48-month KL grade and WOMAC pain (forward), or `kl_max` and `womac_pain_max` (reverse).
- **Metric keys:** `<table>.<exposure>.<outcome>.<term>.<model>`, with `.lo`, `.hi`, `.n` and `.events`.
  - Exposures: `steps`, `mv_min`.
  - Terms: `per_1000`, `band_<level>`, `nonlinear_p`, `baseline`, `baseline_adj`, `change`, `any`, `count`.
- **No causal wording** ("protects", "harms") in any output or in the README results.

## Review Focus

1. **A knee unread at 72 months but read at 96.** Under `roa_unread = "unknown"`, its 72-month ROA is null. It must still contribute one 48→96 period, flagged `span = 2`, rather than being dropped. → Task 2 `test_periods_span_unknown_visit`.
2. **A replacement of a knee that was OA-free at 48 months.** It is an event for incident ROA (`outcomes.roa_replacement = "event"`, as in luyten_early_oa) and for SOA. The knee leaves the risk set afterwards. → Task 2 `test_replacement_is_event_and_stops`.
3. **The two 48-month KL sources disagree.** A knee that is Project 15 KL 1 at V06, but that OUTCOMES99 codes as ROA at or before V06, is excluded from incidence under `kl48.source = "outcomes99"` and included under `"project15"`. → Task 2 `test_kl48_source`.
4. **Person-level inclusion.** Someone valid at 48 months but not at 72 is in P1 and P2 and out of S1, R1 and R2. Someone whose steps are null while their counts are valid is out of the steps models and in S4 (MV). → Task 3 `test_wave_and_steps_inclusion`.
5. **One eligible knee.** A person whose other knee is ROA at 48 months contributes one knee to incidence and that other knee to progression. → Task 2 `test_knee_samples_split`.

### Task 4: Models

**Files:**
- Create: `analyses/steps_oa/models.R` (replacing the placeholder), `covariates.R` (copied from crepitus_activity, extended with a `level = c("knee", "person")` argument that picks `model.covariates_2_knee` or `model.covariates_2_person`), `shape.R`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes:
  - Task 3's frames;
  - `oaimodels::{read_frame, assumptions, fit_knee_gee}`;
  - `geepack::geeglm`, `survival::coxph`.
- Produces:
  - `table_p1.csv`, `table_p2.csv`, `table_s1.csv` … `table_s4.csv`, `table_r1.csv` … `table_r3.csv`. Columns: `table, exposure, outcome, term, model, n, events, estimate, lo, hi`.
  - `curve_p1.csv`, `curve_p2.csv`. Columns: `steps, or, lo, hi`, on a grid from 2,000 to 14,000 by 500, relative to 5,000 steps/day, in the `m1` sample.
  - `metrics_models.csv`.

**Model specifications.**

Each model uses complete cases on its own terms. A fit error, or a band with fewer than 5 events, writes NA.

- **Forward models (P1, P2, S1)**
  - `discrete_time` (default): `fit_knee_gee(periods, "<exposure> + factor(end_visit) + factor(span) + cov", outcome = "event")`, on knee-period rows, clustered on ID.
  - `by_horizon`: the same right-hand side without the period terms, on the horizon frame.
  - Each model has three exposure forms:
    - `steps_48` linear (term `per_1000`);
    - `factor(steps_band, ref = "lt5000")` (terms `band_*`);
    - shape: `shape.R` gives `rcs_basis(x, knots) -> matrix` with two columns (x, and Harrell's one nonlinear term). Its Wald p for the nonlinear coefficient is the term `nonlinear_p`, and its predictions make the curve.
- **P1:** `periods_roa`.
- **P2:** `periods_soa`, run overall and split by `roa` at V06 (outcome labels `soa`, `soa_roa48`, `soa_noroa48`). In `m2`, `kl48` enters as `factor()` for SOA and as 0/1 for ROA.
- **S1:**
  - `periods_*_72` with `steps_48` (term `baseline_adj`) plus `steps_change` (term `change`);
  - wear time at both waves.
- **S2:** `replacement_times` restricted to `incident_roa` knees.
  - Report the events first.
  - If there are at least `tkr.min_events` events: `coxph(Surv(time_yr, event) ~ steps_48 + cov + cluster(ID))`, then the same with `+ steps_change` (people valid at both waves).
  - Otherwise write the events by band only.
- **S3:**
  - Cox as in S2 for `progression_tkr` knees.
  - KL and JSN worsening by V10: GEE logistic, only when `progression.models = "read_knees"` (the default), with outcome label suffix `_read`.
- **S4:** P1 and P2 with `mv_min_48` per 10 min/day, linear only.
- **Reverse models**, person-level, gaussian GEE (independence) with sandwich SEs:
  - **R1:** `steps_72 ~ new_oa_36_48 + steps_48 + cov(48, 72)`.
  - **R2:** `steps_72 ~ new_oa_48_72 + steps_48 + cov(48, 72)`.
  - Term `any`, or `count` under `reverse.coding`. Under `reverse.r2_kl_read`, R2 uses `new_oa_read_48_72`.
- **R3:** descriptive means only, taken from `descriptives.csv`. No model; the table repeats them with SDs.
- **Every model** runs as `m1` and `m2`.

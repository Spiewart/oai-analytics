# steps_oa

GT1M steps at 48 and 72 months and incident and progressive knee OA, in both directions.

**Direction A:** do steps/day at 48 months, and their change to 72 months, predict incident or progressing knee OA (radiographic, symptomatic, knee replacement, measured joint space width) to 96 months?

**Direction B:** does incident or progressing knee OA predict steps at 72 months or their change? This also checks the forward result for reverse causation: knees already worsening could make people walk less.

Knee OA outcomes come from the shared module `src/oai/derive/knee_outcomes.py`.

- **Spec:** [docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-steps-oa-design.md](../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-steps-oa-design.md)
- **Module spec:** [docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-knee-outcomes-module-design.md](../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-knee-outcomes-module-design.md)
- **Plan:** [docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-12-steps-oa.md](../../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-12-steps-oa.md)
- **Assumptions:** [ASSUMPTIONS.md](ASSUMPTIONS.md)

The per-minute steps come from Northwestern (`OAI_STEPS_DIR`, never in the repo). Their terms: the Northwestern analyst who prepared them is a co-author on publications that use them, and the PARR group offers scientific input.

```bash
uv run oai run steps_oa
```

### Reading

- **P1, incident ROA:** each 1,000 steps/day more at 48 months is associated with higher hazard of incident ROA to 96 months: HR 1.06 (1.00–1.13) in Model 1 and 1.07 (1.01–1.13) in Model 2; the Model 1 CI includes 1 and the Model 2 CI excludes it. The 5,000–9,999 bands have HRs of 0.96–0.99 against < 5,000 (all CIs include 1); the ≥ 10,000 band has HR 1.74 (1.01–3.00), so the positive linear estimate is carried by the ≥ 10,000 band. The spline's test of non-linearity gives p = 0.55 (no evidence against a straight line). Across the primary displays, 5 of 32 CIs exclude the null, more than the 1.6 expected by chance at 95%. The Model 1 linear estimate is 1.10 (1.04–1.17) when ROA requires JSN (CI excludes the null); 1.07 (1.00–1.14) after dropping knees with frequent pain at 48 months (CI includes the null); 1.06 (0.99–1.13) in the by-horizon companion, an odds ratio of ROA by 96 months (CI includes the null). To 120 months (provisional, Project 43) the linear HR is 1.02 (0.97–1.07) and the ≥ 10,000 band 1.33 (0.85–2.09), both smaller than at 96 months, with CIs including 1.
- **P2, incident SOA:** HR 0.98 (0.94–1.03) per 1,000 steps/day; its 95% CI includes the null. Split by ROA at 48 months it is 0.96 (0.92–1.02) in knees with ROA (which become SOA through new frequent pain) and 1.08 (1.00–1.17) in knees without ROA (which become SOA mostly through new ROA, as in P1). The split was pre-specified; the two CIs overlap.
- **Secondary families:** 2 of 38 CIs exclude the null, close to the 1.9 expected by chance at 95%. Knee replacement in knees with KL ≥ 2: HR 0.95 (0.89–1.01) (CI includes the null); KL worsening in the read knees: OR 0.99 (0.90–1.10); measured JSW change: −0.006 (−0.027 to +0.014) mm per 1,000 steps/day (CI width 0.041 mm, CI includes the null); MV minutes and incident ROA: HR 1.03 (0.94–1.13) per 10 min/day. No estimate for the change in steps from 48 to 72 months has a CI that excludes the null.
- **Reverse direction.** *R1 (preceding, 36 → 48 months; the window that bears on reverse causation for P1):* 103 people with new ROA, KL worsening or knee replacement before the device wave had, at 72 months, more steps (+236 steps/day, CI −62 to +534; CI includes the null) in Model 1 and more steps (+332 steps/day, CI +1 to +662; CI excludes the null) in Model 2, adjusted for steps at 48 months. These data give no sign that knees already worsening were followed by fewer steps at 72 months; small drops are within the Model 1 CI. *R2 (concurrent, 48 → 72 months):* 103 people with new ROA or replacement between the waves had fewer steps (−156 steps/day, CI −489 to +176; CI includes the null) in Model 1 and fewer steps (−165 steps/day, CI −500 to +170; CI includes the null) in Model 2; this window shows whether steps change alongside new OA, not what preceded the P1 outcomes. *R3 (cross-sectional):* mean steps/day are 6,650 in people whose higher-graded knee is KL 0 and 5,346 in those whose higher-graded knee is KL 4, and 5,617 with SOA against 6,411 without; this gradient by itself does not say which came first.
- **Against the literature** (see Literature): knee replacement in KL ≥ 2 knees (HR 0.95 (0.89–1.01) per 1,000 steps/day) is similar in direction and size to Master 2021's HR of 0.95 (0.84–1.04) (PMID 34175271; different samples: Master's N = 516 had advanced structural OA); KL worsening in the read knees (OR 0.99 (0.90–1.10)) and MV minutes against incident ROA (HR 1.03 (0.94–1.13)) have CIs including 1, as Jayabalan 2019 (PMID 30238649, KL worsening, activity minutes) and Qin 2018 (PMID 28976494, MVPA, incident OA) reported no association; MOST reported no association of steps/day with radiographic worsening in KL 0–2 knees (Øiestad 2015, PMID 26077404). No OAI or MOST paper found here models steps/day against incident ROA by knee with discrete-time follow-up.
- **Design limit:** with two device waves, these models show which measure moves first, not why. Residual confounding (for example by occupation or activity type) is not addressed; knee injury and selective reading are examined post hoc below.
- **Selective readings:** incidence readings are not complete: 1,620 of 1,959 knees at risk of ROA (83%) were read at 96 months, although the design expected incidence readings to be complete. P1 and P2 assume unread knees resemble read ones, and a missed visit is not separated from a film that was not read. In the progression sample only 575 of 1,428 (40%) were read; readings after 48 months were chosen by design (Project 37/42; Project 43 undocumented), so KL and JSN progression is modeled only in the read knees, who differ from the unread. Measured JSW (Project 16) did not follow end-stage knees, and the FNIH 0.7 mm threshold was set for 24–48 months, not the 48 → 96 and 72 → 96-month windows used here.
- **Small cells:** band counts below 5 events appear in S2 tkr_incidence_72; the project treats small cells as advisory, and they are aggregate counts only.
